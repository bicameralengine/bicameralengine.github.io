DATASET 01 · COSMOGENIC PROXY

Beryllium-10

NGRIP / LAW DOME COMPOSITE · 1841–2013

Atmospheric Be-10 concentrations analyzed as an inverse solar proxy. The archived analysis reports 93.8% phase alignment and a T(t) correlation of r = +0.501 (p = 0.048) across the evaluated windows. The record includes documented data-quality considerations.

DATASET 02 · BIOLOGICAL PROXY

Tree-Ring Width

ITRDB NORTHERN HEMISPHERE COMPOSITE · 1841–2012

Tree-ring width analyzed as a downstream biological proxy. The archived analysis reports 93.8% phase alignment and 93.8% transition alignment, while annual correlation does not reach statistical significance.

HELIOSPHERIC OBSERVATIONS
DATASET 03 · WSO / HCS

Heliospheric Current Sheet

WILCOX SOLAR OBSERVATORY · 1976–2025

Observational tilt-angle records and derived evaluation tables for five BSE phase boundaries. Three boundaries meet the study’s quantitative criterion, but the spacing-preserving Monte Carlo test does not establish significance against its primary null model (p = 0.298). The archive supports independent examination of this limited single-record consistency test.
