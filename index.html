Research

The Bicameral Solar Engine (BSE) is a deterministic temporal framework constructed from the mean solar polar magnetic-field reversal interval. It defines a nested harmonic structure for investigating solar-cycle phase relationships and their expression across observational records.

Current research examines solar activity, cosmogenic isotope records, tree-ring data, and possible relationships with long-term economic and historical cycles.
